Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026.

Tabletop Remote does not collect any data. We have no servers that the app talks to, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your device

  • The list of Macs you have connected to, with the names you give them.
  • Your Mac user name and password, if you choose to save them. They are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device and are used only to sign in to your Mac.
  • Your settings and your progress with the retro machines.

Purchases

Remote Desk can be unlocked with a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account, and we keep no record of who has bought it. The app asks the App Store whether the purchase is on your account.

Your Mac's screen

When you connect, the picture of your Mac's screen and your clicks and typing travel directly between your device and your Mac over your own network, using the Screen Sharing feature of macOS. They do not pass through us and we cannot see them.

Permissions

  • Local network: used to find your Mac and connect to it.
  • Face ID or passcode: used to unlock the list of saved Macs on your device. The app never receives your face data.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
