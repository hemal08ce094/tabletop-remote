Support

Questions or a problem? Write to hemalmodi3@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version and macOS version.

Setting up your Mac

  • On the Mac, open System Settings, then General, then Sharing, and turn on Screen Sharing.
  • Keep the Mac and your iPhone or iPad on the same network.
  • In the app, tap Control Your Mac and allow local network access when asked.
  • Pick your Mac from the list and sign in with your Mac user name and password.

My Mac is not in the list

  • Check that Screen Sharing is on and the Mac is awake.
  • Check that local network access is allowed for Tabletop Remote in the Settings app, under Privacy and Security, then Local Network.
  • You can also type the Mac's name or address by hand. The address is shown on the Screen Sharing settings page on the Mac.

The password is not accepted

Use the user name and password of an account on that Mac. If you set a separate Screen Sharing password under "Computer Settings", use that one instead.

Using the trackpad

  • Tap to click. Tap with two fingers to right-click.
  • Drag two fingers to scroll.
  • Touch and hold, then move, to drag.
  • Swipe up with three fingers for the window overview, and sideways to change desktops.

iPhone Duo

Fold the phone halfway and the Mac moves to the top half, with the trackpad and keys on the bottom half. The toolbar hides after a few seconds; tap the round button at the bottom right to bring it back.
